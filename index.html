The Misconception

What most people assume vs. what actually happens

✕ What people think

"I used a bit more this month, so only those extra units should cost more — like income tax brackets."

This is how most people mentally model it. It's not how DISCO billing works for unprotected consumers.

✓ What actually happens

Cross into a higher slab, and the higher rate applies to every single unit you used that month — not just the units above the threshold.

A few extra units can mean your whole bill is recalculated at a rate that's nearly double.
