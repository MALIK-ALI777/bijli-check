How your category is decided

Get this wrong and every other number is wrong.

  • Protected: your 6-month average is 200 units or fewer, no AC is registered on your meter, and this month's usage also stays at or under 200 units.
  • Unprotected: everyone else — including a protected consumer whose usage crosses 200 units in a single month. That one month gets billed at unprotected rates even if every other month was well within the protected range.
  • Non-telescopic billing: for unprotected consumers, the rate for the slab you land in applies to your entire month's units — not just the units above the previous threshold.
  • One-slab benefit: protected consumers get a small cushion — they're billed at the rate of one slab below the one their units actually land in.

Frequently asked

What is FCA and why isn't it in the slab table?▾
Fuel Charge Adjustment reflects month-to-month changes in fuel costs and is set separately from the base slab rate. It changes almost every billing cycle, so there's no fixed number to hardcode — check your DISCO's latest notice or your last bill.
I have an AC — does that always push me to unprotected rates?▾
Yes. Having an AC registered on your meter removes protected-consumer eligibility regardless of how many units you actually use.
Why did my bill jump so much for only a small usage increase?▾
Almost always this is the non-telescopic billing rule — crossing a slab threshold re-rates your entire month's consumption, not just the units above the line. Use the Bill Checker to see exactly how much of the jump is due to this.
Are these numbers guaranteed accurate?▾
These reflect the published domestic tariff schedule as of early 2026. NEPRA revises rates periodically and FCA changes monthly — always treat this as a strong estimate and reference tool, not a replacement for your official duplicate bill.
